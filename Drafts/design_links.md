## Want to Make a new website?
Here are some links to help with that. 
- [HTML](html.md) - foundational language for website coding. 
    - **Pro-tip:** Write most/all of the content for your site first before you start trying to implement layout of any kind. You can do this in either HTML or in [markdown.](markdown.md)
- [CSS](css.md) - The bulding blocks (literally) of your site.
